Toy example: one rule, three verdicts

A hand-authored rule rather than one extracted from a document, because it isolates the thing worth seeing: the same drug, three different amounts of information about the patient, three different verdicts. The rule: an ACE inhibitor should not be started if serum potassium is above 5.0 mmol/L, and potassium should be checked within 14 days of starting one.
